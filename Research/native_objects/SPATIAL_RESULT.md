## What changed and what was proven

The model retains ordinary source-bound requirement IDs and concise meaning. A small
research translation list maps selected static requirements to registered operators:
seated on top, radially outward members, one component at each outward end, aligned
axes, or parallel axes. Explicit endpoints/reference entities/radial plane axes are
hashed with the existing contract and a fixed measurement policy.

Coverage checks actual part/mate/port references and required one-to-one mappings
before geometry. After construction, the checker reads solved solids and verified
feature frames. It rejects side attachment as proof of on-top placement, tangential
members as radial members, reused targets, absent axes, buried ports and misplaced
end interfaces. It does not edit geometry or certify semantic feature selection.

The first live batch (008) exposed two output-schema holes: a radial rule omitted its
axis, and an on-top rule supplied an extraneous reference. Both completed responses
were rejected before planning. Operator-specific structured alternatives corrected
those general defects; fresh 009 responses for the same two requests/roles passed.
The original invalid outputs remain preserved. Tolerances, operators, geometry and
acceptance criteria were not relaxed to obtain the result.

The drone proves the important limit: **pairwise alignment does not establish a
coherent arrangement of the whole object.** It satisfies its declared propeller/motor
pairs while their shared orientation is absent. The checks are useful, but the
inventory can still omit relationships that make the object recognizable and useful.
Do not add a drone-specific preset or claim that another numeric pass solves this.

## Verification and next decision

248 targeted test executions pass, including adversarial spatial measurements,
spending/duplicate guards, unsupported admission, source/contract immutability,
lossless context reconstruction, truthful report output and unchanged geometry
regressions. Seven browser checks pass with zero page errors: load by case URL,
selectable parts, changed explode/isolation images, reset, static motion disabled,
and visible failed-drone construction. Test totals include inherited executions.
Frozen paid runs: `bfd375f` (008), `da4ff9b` (009).

Keep the spatial bridge. Do not spend another repair on the current drone contract.
The next bounded problem is requiring explicit arrangement/axis relationships across
repeated component groups before construction, tested across different object families.
A valid alternative design needs to remain possible; missing group relationships
cannot be silently treated as unconstrained. Preserve unsupported paths/effects
rather than hiding them. Broader component/visual quality still needs its own check;
one coherent simple concept does not clear the general-generation benchmark.
